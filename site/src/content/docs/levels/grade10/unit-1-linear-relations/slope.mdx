> **Question:** How do I recognize and calculate the steepness of a line?

## Think of walking on a hill

Slope describes how much your height changes for each step forward.

## Worked example

**Find the slope of the line through $A(2,\ 3)$ and $B(5,\ 9)$.**

1. Label the points: $(x_1,\ y_1) = (2,\ 3)$ and $(x_2,\ y_2) = (5,\ 9)$.
2. Write the formula: $m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$.
3. Substitute, keeping both subtractions in the same order: $m = \dfrac{9 - 3}{5 - 2}$.
4. Simplify the top and the bottom: $m = \dfrac{6}{3}$.
5. Reduce: $m = 2$.

**Answer:** $m = 2$ — for every 1 unit right, the line rises 2 units.

### A reliable setup

| Point | x | y |
|---|---:|---:|
| A | 2 | 3 |
| B | 5 | 9 |

Subtract in the same direction in the numerator and denominator.

## The formula

$$
m=\frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}
$$

- `m` = slope
- numerator = vertical change
- denominator = horizontal change

## Formal meaning

Slope is the **rate of change** between two points: the change in `y` divided by the change in `x`.

## Use it when

- you are given two points;
- a graph provides two exact points;
- the question asks for slope, steepness, or rate of change;
- you need to compare lines.

If the equation is already `y = mx + b`, the slope is simply the number `m`.

## What the number tells you

| Slope | Picture | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `m > 0` | rises left to right | y increases as x increases |
| `m < 0` | falls left to right | y decreases as x increases |
| `m = 0` | horizontal | no vertical change |
| undefined | vertical | no horizontal change; division by zero |

## Missing-coordinate pattern

If the slope is known, substitute the values and solve.

Example: The slope through `(1, 4)` and `(5, y)` is `3`.

$$
3=\frac{y-4}{5-1}
$$

$$
12=y-4 \quad\Rightarrow\quad y=16
$$

## More worked examples

### Example 2 — Negatives in both subtractions

**Find the slope of the line through $(-2,\ 5)$ and $(4,\ -1)$.**

1. Label: $(x_1,\ y_1) = (-2,\ 5)$ and $(x_2,\ y_2) = (4,\ -1)$.
2. Formula: $m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$.
3. Substitute — subtracting a negative becomes adding: $m = \dfrac{-1 - 5}{4 - (-2)}$.
4. Top: $-1 - 5 = -6$. Bottom: $4 - (-2) = 4 + 2 = 6$.
5. Divide: $m = \dfrac{-6}{6} = -1$.

**Answer:** $m = -1$ — the line falls 1 unit for every 1 unit right.

### Example 3 — A vertical line

**Find the slope of the line through $(3,\ 1)$ and $(3,\ 7)$.**

1. Label: $(x_1,\ y_1) = (3,\ 1)$ and $(x_2,\ y_2) = (3,\ 7)$.
2. Substitute: $m = \dfrac{7 - 1}{3 - 3}$.
3. Simplify: $m = \dfrac{6}{0}$.
4. Dividing by $0$ has no value, so the slope is undefined.
5. Both points share $x = 3$, so the line is vertical.

**Answer:** The slope is **undefined**, and the line is written $x = 3$. Do not confuse this with a *horizontal* line, whose slope is $0$.

## When you see… think…

| When you see… | Think… |
|---|---|
| Two points | `m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)` |
| Rate of change | Slope |
| Line rises left to right | Positive slope |
| Line falls left to right | Negative slope |
| Horizontal line | `m = 0` |
| Vertical line | Undefined slope |

## Common exam mistakes

- Using `run/rise` instead of `rise/run`.
- Subtracting y-values in one direction and x-values in the other.
- Mixing an x-coordinate into the numerator.
- Saying a vertical slope is `0`; it is **undefined**.
- Trusting the drawing instead of using exact coordinates.

## Quick check

1. Find the slope through `(−1, 2)` and `(3, 10)`.
2. What kind of slope does `x = 5` have?
3. A line has slope `−3/4`. Does it rise or fall from left to right?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**1. Slope through $(-1,\ 2)$ and $(3,\ 10)$**

1. Label: $(x_1,\ y_1) = (-1,\ 2)$ and $(x_2,\ y_2) = (3,\ 10)$.
2. Substitute: $m = \dfrac{10 - 2}{3 - (-1)}$.
3. Top: $10 - 2 = 8$. Bottom: $3 - (-1) = 3 + 1 = 4$.
4. Divide: $m = \dfrac{8}{4} = 2$.

**Answer:** $m = 2$.

**2. What kind of slope does $x = 5$ have?**

1. Every point on $x = 5$ has the same x-coordinate, so the run is $0$.
2. The formula gives a denominator of $0$.
3. Dividing by $0$ has no value.

**Answer:** Undefined — the line is vertical.

**3. A line has slope $-\tfrac{3}{4}$. Does it rise or fall from left to right?**

1. The sign of $m$ decides the direction, not its size.
2. $-\tfrac{3}{4}$ is negative.
3. A negative slope goes down as x increases.

**Answer:** It falls.
