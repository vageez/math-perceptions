> **Question:** Which trigonometric ratio connects my angle, known side, and unknown side?

## Think of choosing the right tool

First label the triangle from the angle you are using. Then choose the ratio that contains the two sides in the question.

<TriangleVisual mode="soh" />

## Label before you calculate

Choose the non-right **reference angle** $\theta$.

- **H — Hypotenuse:** opposite the right angle; never changes.
- **O — Opposite:** directly across from $\theta$.
- **A — Adjacent:** beside $\theta$, but not the hypotenuse.

If the reference angle changes, opposite and adjacent swap jobs.

## The three ratios

$$
\sin\theta=\frac{O}{H}\qquad
\cos\theta=\frac{A}{H}\qquad
\tan\theta=\frac{O}{A}
$$

Cross out the side you do not have. The remaining two letters reveal the ratio.

## Worked example — find a side

**A ramp makes a $35°$ angle with the ground and is 4.8 m long. Find its vertical rise.**

1. The ramp is opposite the right angle, so it is `H = 4.8`.
2. The vertical rise is across from `35°`, so it is `O`.
3. The ratio containing O and H is sine: $\sin\theta=O/H$.
4. Substitute: $\sin35°=h/4.8$.
5. Multiply: $h=4.8\sin35°\approx2.75$.

**Answer:** The ramp rises approximately **2.8 m**.

## Finding a side or an angle

| Unknown | Calculator idea | Example form |
|---|---|---|
| Side | Use `sin`, `cos`, or `tan` | $x=12\tan38°$ |
| Angle | Use inverse trig | $\theta=\tan^{-1}(O/A)$ |

`sin⁻¹`, `cos⁻¹`, and `tan⁻¹` mean **inverse functions** here. They undo a ratio to return an angle; they do not mean `1/sin`, `1/cos`, or `1/tan`.

## Worked example — find an angle

**A right triangle has an opposite side of 7 cm and an adjacent side of 10 cm. Find $\theta$.**

1. O and A point to tangent: $\tan\theta=O/A$.
2. Substitute: $\tan\theta=7/10$.
3. Undo tangent: $\theta=\tan^{-1}(7/10)$.
4. In degree mode, $\theta\approx34.99°$.

**Answer:** $\theta\approx35.0°$.

Before pressing equals:

- [ ] Degree mode is on.

## Multi-step pattern

If the needed ratio is missing a side:

1. use Pythagoras or another trig ratio to find that side;
2. keep the unrounded value in the calculator;
3. use it in the next step.

## When you see… think…

| When you see… | Think… |
|---|---|
| Right triangle + non-right angle | SOH CAH TOA |
| O and H | Sine |
| A and H | Cosine |
| O and A | Tangent |
| Need a side | Regular trig |
| Need an angle | Inverse trig |

## Common exam mistakes

- Naming opposite and adjacent before choosing the reference angle.
- Calling a leg the hypotenuse.
- Picking a ratio that does not contain both needed sides.
- Using regular trig to find an angle.
- Leaving the calculator in radian mode.
- Rounding an intermediate result.

## Quick check

1. Relative to angle $\theta$, which side is always opposite the right angle?
2. Which ratio uses adjacent and hypotenuse?
3. Find $\theta$ if `O = 9` and `H = 15`.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. The hypotenuse.
2. Cosine: $\cos\theta=A/H$.
3. $\sin\theta=9/15$, so $\theta=\sin^{-1}(0.6)\approx36.9°$.

</details>
