> Tick a box only after you can do it **without notes**.

## 1. Read the triangle

- [ ] I recognize a right-angle marker.
- [ ] I identify the hypotenuse as the side opposite `90°`.
- [ ] I choose a reference angle before naming opposite and adjacent.
- [ ] I match side `a` with angle `A`, `b` with `B`, and `c` with `C`.
- [ ] I identify the angle included between two sides.
- [ ] I mark known values and the unknown before choosing a method.

## 2. Pythagorean Theorem

- [ ] I use $a^2+b^2=c^2$ only in a right triangle.
- [ ] I find a missing hypotenuse.
- [ ] I find a missing leg.
- [ ] I take the final square root.
- [ ] I check that the hypotenuse is longest.

## 3. SOH CAH TOA

- [ ] I label O, A, and H from the reference angle.
- [ ] I select the ratio containing the known and unknown sides.
- [ ] I find a missing side with regular trig.
- [ ] I find a missing angle with inverse trig.
- [ ] I solve a multi-step right-triangle problem.

## 4. Sine Law

- [ ] I identify a complete known opposite pair.
- [ ] I write matching side–angle ratios.
- [ ] I find a missing side.
- [ ] I find a missing angle using inverse sine.
- [ ] I use $A+B+C=180°$ when needed.
- [ ] I check that larger sides face larger angles.
- [ ] I know my teacher’s expectation for the ambiguous case.

## 5. Triangle area

- [ ] I calculate the semiperimeter correctly.
- [ ] I use Heron’s Formula when three sides are known.
- [ ] I use the SAS formula when two sides and their included angle are known.
- [ ] I keep the whole Heron product under the square root.
- [ ] I include `1/2` and `sin(angle)` in the SAS formula.
- [ ] I report area in square units.

## Recognition speed round

Say the method before calculating:

| Prompt | First move |
|---|---|
| Right triangle; legs 7 and 9; find hypotenuse | Pythagorean Theorem |
| Right triangle; angle `38°`; adjacent known; find opposite | Tangent from SOH CAH TOA |
| `a = 8`, `A = 42°`, `B = 65°`; find `b` | Sine Law |
| Sides 6, 8, and 11; find area | Semiperimeter, then Heron |
| Sides 9 and 12 with included angle `54°`; find area | SAS area formula |

## Five-question readiness check

Try these without notes.

1. Find the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs 8 cm and 15 cm.
2. In a right triangle, `O = 12` and `A = 5`. Find the reference angle.
3. In a triangle, `a = 7`, `A = 40°`, and `B = 75°`. Find `b`.
4. Find the area of a triangle with side lengths 5 m, 5 m, and 6 m.
5. Find the area of a triangle with sides 10 cm and 14 cm enclosing a `30°` angle.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $c=\sqrt{8^2+15^2}=\sqrt{289}=17$ cm.
2. $\theta=\tan^{-1}(12/5)\approx67.4°$.
3. $b=7\sin75°/\sin40°\approx10.5$.
4. $s=8$ and $A=\sqrt{8(3)(3)(2)}=12$ m².
5. $A=\tfrac12(10)(14)\sin30°=35$ cm².

</details>

## Readiness result

- **5 correct:** Ready. Review mistakes once, then rest.
- **3–4 correct:** Nearly ready. Revisit the matching concept page.
- **0–2 correct:** Work through the five pages in order and retry tomorrow.

## Exam habits

- [ ] My calculator is in degree mode.
- [ ] I write the formula before substituting.
- [ ] I keep full precision until the final answer.
- [ ] I show enough steps to earn method marks.
- [ ] I include units and use square units for area.
- [ ] I check whether the result is geometrically possible.
