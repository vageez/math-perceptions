> **Big idea:** The information marked on a triangle tells you which method to use.

## Start by reading the triangle

```text
Is it a right triangle?
  ├─ yes + two sides                 → Pythagorean Theorem
  └─ yes + angle and side            → SOH CAH TOA

Is it any triangle?
  ├─ known side–opposite-angle pair  → Sine Law
  ├─ three sides; need area          → Heron’s Formula
  └─ two sides + included angle      → SAS Area Formula
```

## The recognition map

| When you see… | Think… | First move |
|---|---|---|
| Right angle and two sides | Pythagoras | Mark the hypotenuse `c` |
| Right angle, an angle, and side lengths | SOH CAH TOA | Label O, A, and H from the reference angle |
| A side and the angle directly opposite it | Sine Law | Circle the complete opposite pair |
| Three side lengths and area | Heron | Find the semiperimeter `s` |
| Two side lengths and the angle between them | SAS area | Check that the angle is included |

## Four habits that protect marks

1. **Mark the triangle before choosing a formula.**
2. **Put your calculator in degree mode.**
3. **Keep full precision until the final answer.**
4. **Check the size:** the hypotenuse is longest, larger angles face longer sides, and area uses square units.
