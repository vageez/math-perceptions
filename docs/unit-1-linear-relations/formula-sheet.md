# Unit 1 Formula Sheet

> **Use this page to choose a formula—not just memorize one.**

## Formula map

```text
two points ──→ slope ──→ line equation ──→ compare lines
                │              │                 │
         (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)   y = mx + b    same / neg. reciprocal

two equations ──→ solve together ──→ intersection (x, y)
```

## Core formulas and rules

### 1. Slope from two points

$$
m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}
$$

**Use when:** two points are known and you need steepness or rate of change.

**Remember:** y-change goes on top; x-change goes on bottom. Subtract in the same order.

---

### 2. Slope-intercept form

$$
y=mx+b
$$

**Use when:** reading, writing, or graphing a non-vertical line.

- `m` = slope
- `b` = y-intercept `(0, b)`

**Graph:** start at `b`; move using rise/run from `m`.

**Standard form:** `Ax + By = C`. Rearrange to `y = mx + b` (move the `x` term across, divide every term by the coefficient of `y`) before reading the slope.

---

### 3. Intercepts

| Need | Set | Result |
|---|---|---|
| x-intercept | `y = 0` | point `(x, 0)` |
| y-intercept | `x = 0` | point `(0, y)` |

**Memory hook:** On the x-axis, the y-coordinate is zero.

---

### 4. Special lines

| Line | Equation | Slope |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | `y = c` | `0` |
| Vertical | `x = c` | undefined |

---

### 5. Parallel lines

$$
m_1=m_2
$$

**Use when:** checking or creating parallel lines.

Distinct parallel lines have different intercepts. If the entire equations match, they are the same line.

---

### 6. Perpendicular lines

$$
m_1m_2=-1
$$

Equivalent memory rule: the slopes are **negative reciprocals**.

Example: `3/4 ↔ −4/3`.

Special pair: one horizontal and one vertical line.

---

### 7. Systems of equations

There is no single new formula. The goal is to find `(x, y)` that makes **both** equations true.

| Clue | Choose |
|---|---|
| Need a visual or estimate | Graphing |
| A variable is isolated | Comparison/substitution |
| Coefficients match or can be matched | Elimination |

## Formula selection drill

| If the question gives… | Start with… |
|---|---|
| Two points | Slope formula |
| A slope and one point | `y = mx + b`; substitute to find `b` |
| Two points and asks for an equation | Find `m`, then find `b` |
| `Ax + By = C` and asks for slope | Rearrange to `y = mx + b` |
| An equation and asks for x-intercept | Set `y = 0` |
| A parallel line | Copy the slope |
| A perpendicular line | Negative reciprocal slope |
| Two equations | Choose a systems method |
